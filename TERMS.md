## 3. Hearing safety and limitations

**Do not use maximum volume.** Start quietly and keep volume comfortable, stable and as low as reasonably possible. Stop if you experience pain, ringing, fatigue, dizziness or discomfort. Never increase playback level to hear a missing frequency. Use the car-audio feature only while **parked**.

The software is **not a medical device, audiometric diagnostic, hearing aid, clinical hearing examination, professional speaker calibration system or sound-pressure meter**. All suggested equalizer gains are subjective estimates and may be inaccurate, unsuitable or perceptually ineffective for your hearing, music or device. Digital dBFS levels do not describe your actual exposure in dB SPL. Do not rely on this product for healthcare decisions; consult a licensed hearing-care professional for medical concerns.
